# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

The first version.
- A streamer's deck beside their Twitch stream: what's left, the odds of drawing each card, the
  opponent's cards seen and Legends, and the last card played.
- Card hovers: point at a card on the streamer's table in the video to see it full size.
- Keeps to the video: updates wait for Twitch's player, plus your own extra delay if you want it.
